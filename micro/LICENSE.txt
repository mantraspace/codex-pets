Definition of Micro

For the purposes of this notice, "Micro" refers to the original custom
Codex pet created and developed by Franck Gosselin.

A custom Codex pet is a user-created animated character designed for
use with Codex, OpenAI's AI coding agent. It is represented within
Codex through a pet manifest and associated visual assets and
animations.

Micro includes its character design, visual appearance, sprite
artwork, animations, spritesheet, pet manifest, documentation, and
the original files distributed as part of the Micro pet package.

Micro is depicted as a retro toy microcomputer with a beige case,
green screen eyes, keyboard, and waving coiled cable.

References to "Micro" in this notice include the Micro character and
the original Micro pet package, except where the context clearly
indicates otherwise.
